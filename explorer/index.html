castiling

en passent

this is a move where if whites pawn is on the the 5th rank and if black pushes their pawn by 2 squares and their pawn is side by side with your pawn then you can move diagonally towards the black pawn and capture the black pawn.

you can do the same with black if blacks panwn is on the 4th rank and white pushes their pawn by 2 squares black en passent.

note:if you dont do en passent right after the opponet moves the pawn en passant cant be possible.
